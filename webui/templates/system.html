Apply updates

Runs dnf update -y on the appliance host. This can take several minutes and may briefly restart the appliance's own services (web UI, renewal timer) if their underlying packages (Python, gunicorn, etc.) are updated. Progress is written to the Logs page.

Requires step-up authentication: in addition to your web UI login, applying updates requires the username and password of a real Linux account in the appliance's {{ sudo_group }} (sudo-capable) group. This password is used only to verify your identity for this one action -- it is never stored, logged, or used for anything else.
